Data safety under load

  • No lost data: 110 notes written by users at the same time, 110 stored.

How it was tested

  • Test data: 10,000 leads, 50,000 timeline entries, 5,000 follow-ups, 2,000 clients, 2,000 units; 10 users across all six roles. Separate test database, not live data.
  • Production build of the CRM. Pages measured signed in as Super Admin, who sees the most data.
  • p50 = typical speed (half of requests are faster). p95 = 95 of every 100 requests are faster than this.
  • Machine: development laptop (Intel i5, 16 GB RAM), with the CRM, database and test tool on the same machine. Results on the final server may differ and will be rechecked at handover.
