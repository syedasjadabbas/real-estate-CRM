# External integrations: excluded from the current delivery

Prepared 3 Nov 2026.

**None of the integrations below is in the signed PKR 175,000 quotation:**
- The feature list contains no integration.
- The exclusions make third-party API and usage charges the client's.
- "any additional feature, integration … will be reviewed and quoted separately before work begins".

They are **not part of the Version 1 handover**. None will be started without the client's written request, a separate quotation, and Zeploy Tech's explicit approval.

The permission and product names below follow each platform's documentation as generally known. **They must be re-checked against the platform's current documentation when an integration is quoted**, because platforms change access rules.

---

## 1. Meta (Facebook / Instagram) Lead Ads → CRM leads (FR-040)
- **What it would do:** new Lead Ads form submissions arrive as CRM leads with inbound channel "Meta" and the Meta lead ID stored, so duplicates are detected.
- **Client must provide:**
  - **Business Manager:** a verified Meta Business Manager that owns the Facebook Page(s) and ad account(s), with an admin who can grant access.
  - **Page admin:** admin role on each Page that runs lead forms.
  - **Meta app:** a Meta for Developers app owned by the client's business.
    - Permissions typically needed for lead retrieval: `leads_retrieval`, `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement`, `pages_manage_metadata`, `pages_manage_ads`.
    - These usually require **App Review** and **Business Verification**, which can take days to weeks.
  - **Token:** a long-lived Page access token or System User token, generated by the client's admin.
  - **Privacy policy:** a public privacy-policy URL, required by Meta.
  - **Field mapping:** the lead forms and their questions, to map onto CRM fields (name, phone, city, project, budget).
- **Technical prerequisites on our side:**
  - a public HTTPS webhook on the CRM domain;
  - webhook signature verification with the app secret (`SECURITY_AND_PRIVACY.md` §7: "verify signatures; reject unsigned requests");
  - a verify token;
  - duplicate handling by phone and Meta lead ID;
  - an audit row per ingested lead.
- **Costs:** none from Meta for retrieving leads. Ad spend is the client's.

## 2. TikTok Lead Generation → CRM leads (FR-041)
- **What it would do:** TikTok instant-form leads become CRM leads with inbound channel "TikTok", raw payload kept.
- **Client must provide:**
  - **Ads account:** TikTok for Business / Ads Manager advertiser account, with admin access.
  - **Developer app:** a TikTok for Business developer app with Marketing API / Leads access approved, owned by the client.
  - **IDs and token:** the advertiser ID and a long-lived access token authorised by the client's admin.
  - **Field mapping:** the instant forms and fields.
- **Technical prerequisites:** a webhook or scheduled lead fetch, signature or secret verification, duplicate handling, audit.

## 3. WhatsApp (messages to leads or clients)
- **What it would do:** not defined. It would need a requirements discussion (templates, triggers, opt-in).
- **Client must provide:**
  - a **verified** Meta Business Manager;
  - a **WhatsApp Business Account**;
  - a dedicated phone number not used in the WhatsApp app;
  - a System User permanent token;
  - approved message templates;
  - an opt-in and consent process.
- **Costs:** per-conversation charges billed by Meta to the client.

## 4. Email sending (notifications, password resets) (Q-T2)
- **Client must provide:**
  - an email provider account (e.g. a transactional email service, or SMTP);
  - a sending domain with DNS access for SPF, DKIM and DMARC records;
  - the from-address.
- **Note:** notifications are in-app only in Version 1 (N6 open).

## 5. SMS
- Not requested.
- Would need an SMS gateway account, sender ID registration (PTA rules in Pakistan), and per-message charges paid by the client.

## 6. AI call analysis (FR-039)
- **Explicitly excluded** in the requirements (separate quote, client-paid AI API).
- **Client must provide:**
  - an AI provider account and API key;
  - written approval to send call recordings to a third party (privacy);
  - data-retention terms.

## 7. Telephony / call data
- Calls use normal SIM phones with manual recording upload. There is no telephony API.
- Call metrics stay "not available" unless the client decides uploaded recordings count as calls (C-19). That is an in-app rule, not an integration.

## 8. Payment gateways, bank integrations, tax, payroll, commissions
- Excluded by the quotation ("Advanced accounting, tax filing, bank integrations, payment gateway integrations, automated commissions … are not included unless expressly listed").

## 9. Maps
- Not requested.
- The proposal floor map (CR-2) uses uploaded plan images, **not** a maps service.

## 10. Data migration from SaleStorm (Q-P5, FR-043 / FR-075)
- Not an external integration, but also outside this handover.
- **Client must provide:** CSV exports of leads, units and bookings from the old system, and confirm that they hold the right to move that data.
- Import scripts would be quoted and tested separately on a copy, never directly in production.

---

## Approval rule
For any item above, the steps are:
1. the client's written request;
2. the dependencies above delivered;
3. a separate quotation approved;
4. Zeploy Tech's explicit go-ahead.

Only then does work begin. Nothing in this document authorises starting an integration.
