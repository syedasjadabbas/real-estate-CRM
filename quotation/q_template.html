Modules & Features Included

The following modules and feature names define the quoted Version 1 scope. Any item not listed here is outside the agreed scope unless approved separately.

Dashboard

  • Live analytics dashboard with real-time data
  • Pipeline KPIs: total leads, open, won, lost and conversion rate
  • Re-inquiry (regenerated leads) tracking
  • Pipeline health: lead age vs meetings, stale leads
  • Lead source and inbound channel breakdown
  • Top projects by lead volume and recent leads
  • Date range filters (24h, 7d, 30d, MTD, 6m, custom)
  • Project switcher, follow-up alerts and notifications

Leads

  • All leads table with search, filters and sorting
  • Sales pipeline: Kanban board, table view and analytics
  • Pipeline stages from New Lead to Booked, Lost and Future Opportunity
  • Lead views: To-dos, Favorites, Reviews, Won, Lost, Regenerated, Shared phones and Duplicates
  • Add leads manually or capture from Meta and TikTok lead forms
  • Automatic lead assignment (rotation) with unique lead codes
  • Lead profile with full timeline, notes and stage history
  • Follow-up scheduling with due reminders
  • Call recording and document uploads with AI call analysis
  • Start a booking directly from a lead

Clients

  • Long-term client profiles
  • Investment history
  • Contracts and payment history
  • Relationship status
  • Next follow-up tracking
  • Search, filters and sorting

Projects

  • Project portfolio (building, farmhouse, society)
  • Project type, developer and active / inactive status
  • Inventory dimensions per project (floors, block, size, category, type, premium)
  • Units tracked per project
  • Direct link to project inventory

Inventory

  • Unit listing by project, floor and unit number
  • Area, base price and per sq ft pricing
  • Sale model: whole unit or SPU (fractional share)
  • Unit nature, category, type and bedrooms
  • Unit status: available, reserved, sold
  • Unit detail page with full dimensions
  • Bulk inventory import (CSV)
  • Filters, sorting and pagination

Bookings

  • New booking: select project, lead and available unit
  • Per sq ft rate and token amount
  • Automatic unit hold based on token, auto-release on expiry
  • Booking stages: negotiation, forms and payments, confirmed, cancelled
  • Payment tracking (paid vs total) and 30% milestone
  • Payment instructions shown to buyer
  • Provisional receipt and sales order
  • Agreement and payment document status

Tasks

  • Create, assign and track tasks
  • Task type, status, due date and assignee
  • Link tasks to leads and bookings
  • Printable investment proposal and payment plan
  • Unit pricing auto-fill, capped discount and installment schedule
  • Filters, sorting and pagination

Meetings

  • Schedule and manage meetings
  • Date and time, organizer and linked lead
  • Meeting status tracking
  • Meetings counted in pipeline and performance reports
  • Filters and sorting

Targets

  • Daily, weekly and monthly targets
  • Per-agent targets: calls, meetings and sold sq ft
  • Period and anchor date selection
  • Set and edit agent targets

Teams

  • Sales team management
  • Team members and lead assignment (active / inactive teams)
  • Team performance
  • Manager daily report
  • Duty roster

HR

  • Employee directory: roles, departments, contacts and profiles
  • Holiday calendar (company-wide and regional)
  • Leave requests and approvals
  • Annual and CPL leave with balances
  • Employee activity

My Workplace

  • My leave requests and status
  • Upcoming company holidays
  • CRM sign-in and sign-out log

Performance

  • Agent KPIs: open leads, booked, lost and win %
  • Calls and talk minutes from call recordings
  • Follow-ups due vs done (follow-up %)
  • Meetings per agent
  • Call quality (verified and qualified calls)
  • Charts: outcome mix, top closers, call volume, calls vs meetings
  • CRM attendance (sign-ins and sign-outs)
  • Critical agent flag and date range filters

Accounts & Finance (additional module)

  • Income records
  • Expense records
  • Booking payments
  • Outstanding balances
  • Payment history
  • Financial reports
